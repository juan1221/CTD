01

RD 360° Strategic Landing

Enter the Dominican Republic with certainty.

Comprehensive market-entry advisory including regulatory assessment, stakeholder mapping, operational setup, commercial strategy and executive integration support.

Market-entry advice and executive landing support that connect an opportunity with local context, required capabilities and a practical entry roadmap.

What the offering does

An attractive opportunity is only the starting point. CTD helps companies and investors understand the Dominican environment, define an entry strategy, map relevant stakeholders and navigate the local ecosystem. The mandate connects strategic analysis with the practical decisions required to establish a presence.

Who it serves

Multinationals, investment funds, family offices, tour operators, developers, and infrastructure or service companies exploring entry into the Dominican Republic.

Services within the mandate

  • • Country & Opportunity Briefing: Frame the country context and opportunity before committing to an entry direction.
  • • Entry Strategy and Roadmap: Define priorities, required capabilities and the next decisions.
  • • Stakeholder Mapping: Identify the relevant local stakeholders and ecosystem relationships for the mandate.
  • • Executive Landing: Support executive arrival and initial market navigation.
  • • Local Ecosystem Navigation: Help interpret the local environment as the entry plan develops.
  • • Post-Landing Advisory: Continue advisory support after the initial landing within an agreed scope.
  • • Post-Landing Advisory: Continue advisory support after the initial landing within an agreed scope.

A Dominican Republic Entry Roadmap identifying priorities, required capabilities and next decisions.

02

Integrated Security Master Plan

Security should begin with the design.

Integrated security planning that aligns physical protection, technology, specialist interfaces and operating requirements with the development.

What the offering does

CTD develops a coherent security architecture for complex developments rather than treating individual systems as separate purchases. The master plan connects the asset design, user experience and future operations, with specialist coordination where required.

Who it serves

Resorts, branded residences, tourism developments, mixed-use projects, ports, airports and corporate campuses.

Services within the mandate

  • • Physical, electronic and perimeter security requirements: Establish the protection requirements that the design must address.
  • • Access control planning: Align access requirements with the users and operating model of the development.
  • • CCTV planning: Define surveillance requirements as part of the wider security architecture.
  • • Command-and-control planning: Connect security systems with operational coordination requirements.
  • • Cyber, fire and life-safety coordination: Coordinate interfaces with the relevant specialists rather than imply a single discipline covers every requirement.
  • • Operating protocols and resilience requirements: Align the planned architecture with how the asset will function.

A Security Master Plan aligning protection requirements with the asset, user experience and operations.

04

Critical Infrastructure Risk Advisory

Protect the asset. Preserve operations.

Risk and resilience advice that connects asset vulnerabilities, existing controls and modernization priorities with operational continuity.

What the offering does

CTD helps owners and operators of critical infrastructure understand the risks to their assets, operations and users. The mandate connects asset vulnerabilities with operational continuity requirements, modernization priorities and resilience planning.

Who it serves

Airports, ports, utilities, energy, water, telecommunications and other critical infrastructure operators.

Services within the mandate

  • • Risk & Vulnerability Assessment: Assess the relationship between asset vulnerabilities and operational exposure.
  • • Security Architecture Review: Review existing protection arrangements and controls against the needs of the operation.
  • • Modernization Roadmap: Connect risk priorities with investment choices and resilience requirements.
  • • Continuity & Crisis Advisory: Advise on continuity and crisis readiness within explicitly agreed boundaries.
  • • Independent Strategic Advisory: Provide a risk-led perspective on priorities before selecting technology or interventions.

Risk-based priorities and a modernization roadmap connecting controls, investment and operational resilience. Final outputs depend on the agreed assessment and advisory scope.

Starting conversation

What must be protected? Against what risk? What level of resilience is required? Where should investment be prioritized?

Product distinction

The Integrated Security Master Plan centers on coherent security design for a development. This offering centers on asset risk, existing architecture and continuity priorities for essential operations.

05

Protection fits the way your family lives

Discreet risk advice for families, executives and their assets, aligning protection planning with privacy, lifestyle and existing arrangements.

What the offering does

CTD frames a private-client mandate around the people and residences within scope, how the family travels, and the providers and protocols already in place. The purpose is to integrate protection while respecting privacy and lifestyle. Detailed information should be shared only after appropriate arrangements and assessment boundaries are agreed.

Who it serves

Families, executives and family offices seeking a coordinated approach to private-client risk.

Proposed service modules

The retrieved brochure supports the advisory purpose and discovery approach but did not expose the complete private-client service list. The following explanatory modules are proposed scope language, not confirmed package inclusions.

  • • Private-client scoping: Agree which people, residences and assets the mandate addresses.
  • • Residential protection review: Review existing arrangements for residences within the agreed scope.
  • • Mobility and travel risk advice: Define advisory needs around the family’s travel and movement patterns.
  • • Provider and protocol coordination: Clarify how existing providers and procedures fit together.
  • • Protection-planning priorities: Agree a coordinated planning approach that respects privacy and lifestyle.

Starting conversation

Which people and residences fall within scope? How does the family travel? What providers and protocols are already in place?
